CSL CAD Viewer

PROFESSIONAL CAD EDITION
Version 1.1.9 CSL V0.0.1
Built for Cochin Shipyard Limited

Cochin Shipyard Limited is one of India’s leading shipbuilding and ship repair organizations, supporting complex marine engineering, fabrication, repair, and modernization programs. This viewer is tailored for engineering review, fabrication coordination, and production monitoring in shipyard environments.

It is intended to help teams review large 3D models quickly, inspect structural geometry, validate section cuts, and support field and office collaboration across engineering workflows.
